<Eyebrow>1 · Where review & reporting fit</Eyebrow>
## 1.1 In the F8 exam
Review & Reporting (syllabus Part E) is short on volume but high on traps. The examiner reuses the same 4–5 decision points across papers. Master those decision points and you secure consistent 5-mark sub-parts in Section B plus 2–4 MCQs in Section A.

| Where in the exam | Format | Typical task | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section B — 5-mark sub-part | Constructed response | "Discuss and explain the effect on the auditor's report if [issue] is not [recognised/disclosed]" | 5 marks — 1 mark per: materiality calc, opinion type, basis paragraph, report section, justification |
| Section B — 5–10 marks | Constructed response | Substantive procedures for: subsequent events / going concern indicators / specific provision | 1 mark per well-written procedure |
| Section A — MCQs | Multi-choice / multi-response | Adjusting vs non-adjusting · GC indicators · opinion type · MURGC vs KAM vs EOM distinction | 2 marks per MCQ |

Pattern: every F8 paper since 2018 has tested at least ONE auditor's report opinion-choice question. Knowing the decision tree (Section 2) is non-negotiable.

## 1.2 The audit completion roadmap
| Stage | What happens | ISA / Output |
|---|---|---|
| ① Fieldwork complete | Substantive procedures finished. Misstatements identified, accumulated, evaluated. | ISA 450 — Evaluation of Misstatements |
| ② Final review procedures | Subsequent events review · Going concern assessment · Final analytical procedures · Written representations · Other information review. | ISA 560 · ISA 570 · ISA 520 · ISA 580 · ISA 720 |
| ③ Form the opinion | Apply the decision tree (Section 2) to all uncorrected misstatements + scope limitations + GC issues. | ISA 700 / 705 / 706 / 701 |
| ④ Issue auditor's report | Sign and date the report. Reporting date = end of active duty for subsequent events. | ISA 700 — Forming an opinion |
| ⑤ Post-issue events | Passive duty: act only if new facts come to attention. May require reissue of FS + new auditor's report, OR steps to prevent reliance on the original report. | ISA 560 — paragraphs 10–17 |

Critical insight: the WAY you handle each stage (procedures performed, judgements made) is what gets tested in Section B. The TIME WINDOW of subsequent events (stages 4 vs 5) is the most common SE trap.

## 1.3 Two skills tested simultaneously
| Skill | Trigger words in the requirement | How to answer |
|---|---|---|
| Skill A — PERFORM procedures | "Describe substantive procedures" · "Explain how the auditor would verify" · "Procedures to confirm" | Apply Checkpoint 4 risk-driven workflow: Risk → Assertion at risk → Procedure (3W formula). |
| Skill B — CHOOSE opinion | "Discuss and explain the effect on the auditor's report" · "What is the appropriate opinion" · "How should the auditor report" | Apply the decision tree (Section 2). <Eyebrow>2 · The master decision tree</Eyebrow>
## 2.1 Three questions, five outcomes
Adapted from ACCA Technical Article "Forming an audit opinion" (ISA 700 / 705). **Q1.** Is the issue a MATERIAL MISSTATEMENT in the FS (recognised or omitted) OR an INABILITY to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence (scope limitation)? NO → ✔ UNMODIFIED OPINION. **Q2.** Is the matter MATERIAL? (Benchmarks: 5% of PBT; 0.5–1% of revenue; 1–2% of total assets.) NO → ✔ UNMODIFIED. **Q3.** Is the matter PERVASIVE? (ISA 705: ≥1 of 3 tests met.) Combine with the nature of the issue:

| Nature of issue ↓ / Pervasiveness → | NOT pervasive (material only) | PERVASIVE (material AND pervasive) |
|---|---|---|
| Material MISSTATEMENT in FS (directors recorded the wrong number OR omitted a required disclosure) | **QUALIFIED OPINION** "Except for the effects of the matter described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion section, the FS present fairly…" | **ADVERSE OPINION** "The FS DO NOT present fairly…" (the misstatement is so material and pervasive that the FS as a whole are misleading) |
| INABILITY to obtain SAAE (scope limitation: records destroyed, mgmt refuses access, auditor cannot verify) | **QUALIFIED OPINION** "Except for the possible effects of the matter described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion section, the FS present fairly…" | **DISCLAIMER OF OPINION** "We DO NOT express an opinion on the FS" (auditor cannot conclude at all — possible effects could be both material and pervasive) |

Memorisation aid: NO-MIS-NO-LIM → UNMODIFIED · NOT-MATERIAL → UNMODIFIED · MIS + NOT pervasive → QUALIFIED "except for" · MIS + pervasive → ADVERSE · LIM + NOT pervasive → QUALIFIED "possible effects" · LIM + pervasive → DISCLAIMER. Every modified opinion is preceded by a "Basis for [Qualified / Adverse / Disclaimer of] Opinion" paragraph that quantifies the issue. Forgetting that Basis paragraph in your answer = automatic −1 mark.

## 2.2 Pervasiveness test
| Criterion (any one suffices) | Plain-English test |
|---|---|
| ① Not confined to specific elements, accounts or items | The issue affects multiple line items across the FS (e.g., wrong GC basis affects every asset and liability). |
| ② If confined, represents or could represent a substantial proportion of the FS | The issue touches a single line, but that line is huge relative to the FS (e.g., 40% of total assets misstated). |
| ③ For disclosures, fundamental to users' understanding | A missing disclosure that — without it — users cannot understand the FS (e.g., not disclosing a going-concern uncertainty). |

Quick rule of thumb for exam: misstatements >10% of PBT and touching multiple balances = pervasive candidate. Single-line misstatement at 5–10% of PBT = material but not pervasive.

## 2.3 Auditor's report sections
| Section of the report | When it appears | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| Opinion section | ALWAYS — first substantive section | States whether FS give a true and fair view. Wording changes with opinion type (unmodified / qualified / adverse / disclaimer). |
| Basis for [Opinion type] | ALWAYS (just below Opinion) | For modified opinions: explains the misstatement / scope limitation + quantifies it. For unmodified: states the audit was conducted under ISAs. |
| Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern (MURGC) | When GC is appropriate BUT material uncertainty exists AND adequately disclosed | Separate section under ISA 570. Refers users to the FS note on the uncertainty. Opinion stays UNMODIFIED. |
| Key Audit Matters (KAM) | LISTED entities ONLY (ISA 701) | Matters of most significance to the current audit. NOT a substitute for modification, MURGC, or EOM. |
| Emphasis of Matter (EOM) | Optional — matter ALREADY DISCLOSED in FS that auditor wants to highlight | Draws attention to a note in the FS. Does NOT modify the opinion. Example: subsequent reissue of FS. |
| Other Matter (OM) | Optional — matter NOT in the FS but relevant to users | Examples: prior period unaudited · restricted distribution. Does NOT modify the opinion. |

BIG TRAP: MURGC, KAM, EOM, OM are NOT modifications — they appear with an UNMODIFIED opinion. Calling them "modifications" in your answer = lost mark.

## 2.4 The 3-step answer formula
| Step | What to write | Marks earned |
|---|---|---|
| ① Identify & QUANTIFY the issue | Name the issue (e.g., "redundancy provision of $1.8m not recognised") + calculate materiality (% of PBT, % of revenue, % of net assets). State material/not material conclusion. | 1–2 marks |
| ② Apply the DECISION TREE | "This is a misstatement / inability to obtain SAAE" → "It is material but not pervasive / material AND pervasive" → state the opinion type. | 1–2 marks |
| ③ Describe REPORT SECTIONS affected | Name the sections that change: Opinion section + Basis for [type] Opinion paragraph (state where it appears: BELOW the Opinion section). For unmodified-with-extras: name MURGC / EOM / OM section + content. | 1–2 marks |

## 3.1 Subsequent events (ISA 560 + IAS 10)
Risk lens: events between YE and report issue date can change the FS, but only some require adjustment. Misclassifying an event is the most common SE exam error.

| Step | Question | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| STEP 1 — Classify | Did the CONDITION exist at YE? (i.e., the event after YE merely provides EVIDENCE of YE condition) | YES = ADJUSTING event (must adjust FS). NO = NON-ADJUSTING (disclose if material). |
| STEP 2 — Materiality | Is the event material to the FS? (use percent of PBT / net assets thresholds) | NOT material = no action. Material = continue. |
| STEP 3 — Directors action | For adjusting: did directors adjust? For non-adjusting: did directors disclose? | YES = unmodified opinion. NO = modified opinion (apply 2x2 matrix from Section 2). |
| STEP 4 — Timing | When was the event discovered relative to the report sign date? | Before sign = active duty. After sign, before issue = passive duty. After issue = prevent reliance. |

| Symptom in scenario | Classification | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Customer files for insolvency post-YE; receivable outstanding at YE | ADJUSTING | Customer financial difficulty existed at YE, confirms YE bad debt allowance is insufficient. |
| Defective product batch made BEFORE YE; defect discovered AFTER YE | ADJUSTING | Defect (impaired inventory NRV) existed at YE. |
| Legal case settled post-YE for amount different from YE provision | ADJUSTING | Provides evidence of the YE liability correct amount. |
| Share issue, dividend declared, acquisition announced AFTER YE | NON-ADJUSTING | New condition arose after YE. Disclose if material. |
| Fire / flood / explosion destroying assets AFTER YE | NON-ADJUSTING | New condition arose after YE. Disclose if material. |
| Major customer announces cash-flow problems AFTER YE; receivable at YE | ADJUSTING | Customer cash-flow problem typically existed before YE, confirms recoverability concern. |

| Time window | Auditor duty | Trigger | Required action |
|---|---|---|---|
| YE to date of audit report | ACTIVE duty | Identify subsequent events through procedures (enquiry, board minutes, post-YE management accounts, written rep). | Adjust / disclose / amend the report as needed before signing. |
| Date of report to date FS issued | PASSIVE duty | Auditor only acts if facts come to attention that, had they been known at report date, would have caused amendment. | Discuss with mgmt. If FS amended, perform new procedures; date new report no earlier than amended FS. |
| After FS issued | PASSIVE duty + Prevent reliance | New facts emerge after FS issued and report would have been different. | Discuss with mgmt. Mgmt should reissue FS; auditor issues new report with EOM on reissue. If mgmt refuses, take steps to prevent reliance. |

## 3.2 Going concern (ISA 570 Revised)
Risk lens: GC is THE most-tested Reporting topic. Two judgements: (1) Is GC basis appropriate? (2) Is there a material uncertainty? Five distinct opinion outcomes.

| Situation | Disclosure adequate? | Auditor report |
|---|---|---|
| GC basis APPROPRIATE, no material uncertainty | N/A | UNMODIFIED. No extra section. |
| GC basis APPROPRIATE, BUT material uncertainty exists | Adequate | UNMODIFIED opinion + "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section (NOT a KAM, NOT a modification). |
| GC basis APPROPRIATE, BUT material uncertainty exists | Inadequate | MODIFIED — QUALIFIED or ADVERSE opinion (material misstatement of disclosures). |
| GC basis INAPPROPRIATE, but FS still prepared on GC basis | N/A | ADVERSE opinion (whole basis of preparation is wrong = pervasive). |
| GC unclear, management UNWILLING to extend assessment | N/A | QUALIFIED or DISCLAIMER (inability to obtain SAAE). |

BIG TRAP 1: When disclosure is adequate, opinion stays UNMODIFIED. Many candidates wrongly say "qualified" — the modification only happens if disclosure is missing or inadequate. BIG TRAP 2: GC assessment period is 12 months from THE DATE OF THE FS, not from the auditor report date.

| Category | Indicators | NOT indicators (common traps) |
|---|---|---|
| Financial | Loan covenant breached · Overdraft renewal refused · Negative operating cash flow · Major debt repayable shortly with no plan · Supplier credit withdrawn · Dividend arrears | Existence of long-term loan with no breach · Decrease in inventory days (improvement in cash cycle) |
| Operating | Loss of key customer · Loss of key staff (CFO, MD) · Major regulatory non-compliance · Withdrawal of license · Strikes / industrial action | Departure of a single non-finance director · Reorganisation that is funded |
| Other | Major litigation with potentially disastrous outcome · Uninsured catastrophic loss · Pending changes in law that will adversely affect entity | Routine legal proceedings within insurance cover |

## 3.3 Written representations (ISA 580)
Risk lens: written reps are SUPPLEMENTARY evidence, never a substitute for SAAE. Refusal to provide is itself a scope limitation.

| Aspect | Key point |
|---|---|
| Always required | Management responsibilities (preparation of FS, internal control, providing complete information) · All transactions recorded · Subsequent events have been adjusted or disclosed. |
| Cannot substitute for | Other audit evidence. If a balance is material, written rep alone is insufficient. |
| If management REFUSES | Treat as inability to obtain SAAE. Pervasive = DISCLAIMER. Not pervasive = QUALIFIED. |

## 3.4 Other information (ISA 720)
OI = the narrative parts of the annual report (chair statement, directors report, MD and A) — NOT the audited FS. Auditor must read OI for material inconsistency, but does NOT audit it.

| Scenario | Effect on opinion | Report section affected |
|---|---|---|
| OI is consistent with FS | UNMODIFIED — no comment needed beyond standard "Other Information" section confirming no inconsistency identified | Standard "Other Information" section. |
| Material inconsistency in OI, directors REFUSE to correct | UNMODIFIED opinion — the FS themselves are correct | "Other Information" section describes the uncorrected material misstatement of OI. |

TRAP: many candidates wrongly modify the opinion when OI is wrong. The opinion is on the FS — if FS are fine, opinion stays unmodified, regardless of what the chair report says.

## 3.5 Modifications vs unmodified-with-extras
| Concept | Opinion type | Triggered by | Report section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modified opinion | Qualified / Adverse / Disclaimer (CHANGES opinion) | Material misstatement OR inability to obtain SAAE | Opinion section is reworded + Basis for [Type] Opinion paragraph added. |
| MURGC | UNMODIFIED (unchanged) | Material uncertainty re going concern + adequate disclosure | Separate "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section. |
| Emphasis of Matter (EOM) | UNMODIFIED (unchanged) | Matter ALREADY disclosed in FS that auditor wants to highlight (e.g., reissue, major litigation outcome uncertain but disclosed) | Separate "Emphasis of Matter" section after Basis for Opinion. |
| Key Audit Matters (KAM) | UNMODIFIED (unchanged) | Matters of most significance in audit (LISTED entities only, ISA 701) | Separate "Key Audit Matters" section listing 1–3 matters with how addressed. |

Rule of thumb: if the answer to "did the opinion change?" is NO, you are dealing with an unmodified-with-extras situation. Use the right section name (MURGC / EOM / KAM) — do NOT call it a modification.
