## The Audit Ecosystem
Central concept: External Audit → provides assurance → reliability of financial statements.

| External audit (Ch 1) | Internal audit (Ch 4) |
|---|---|
| Purpose: Express independent opinion on FS | Purpose: Evaluate internal controls & add value to organisation |
| Appointed by: Shareholders (AGM) | Appointed by: Management / Audit Committee |
| Reports to: Shareholders | Reports to: Those Charged with Governance |
| Mandatory for public interest entities | Voluntary (recommended for larger entities) |
| ISA-governed; statutory requirements | IIA Standards; more flexible scope |
| Cannot perform management functions | Can be outsourced to third-party firms |

## Corporate Governance & Audit Committee (Ch 2–3)
| Corporate governance | Audit committee | Engagement basics |
|---|---|---|
| System by which companies are directed & controlled | Board sub-committee of NEDs (Non-Executive Directors) | Engagement letter: confirms terms before audit starts |
| Board responsibility: financial reporting, internal control, risk management | Role: Oversee internal audit, financial reporting, external audit independence | Letter of representation: written confirmation from management at end |
| Directors' responsibilities: prepare FS giving true & fair view | Monitor integrity of FS, review risk management systems | Professional clearance: must obtain from previous auditor |
| Audit committees: monitor reliability of FS, external audit relationship | Recommend appointment/removal of external auditor to shareholders | Preconditions: must agree audit basis, management responsibilities |

## Appointment, Removal & Liability (Ch 1)
| Topic | Key rules |
|---|---|
| Appointment | First auditor: appointed by directors (before first AGM). Subsequent: shareholders at AGM. |
| Removal | By ordinary resolution (shareholders) with special notice (28 days). Auditor can attend AGM & address shareholders. |
| Resignation | Auditor must deposit statement of circumstances at company registered office. No suppression of matters shareholders should know. |
| Rights of auditor | Access to books/records; receive info from officers; attend/speak at general meetings. |
| Liability | Unlimited liability at law (unless limited liability partnership). Cannot contract out of liability for negligence. |
| True & Fair View | Overall FS must present a true and fair view. Auditor must consider this even if individual items comply with IFRS. |

<Eyebrow>2 · Exam techniques</Eyebrow>
## Section A MCQ approach
| Question type | Approach | Common trap |
|---|---|---|
| Definition of audit | Focus on "independent" + "opinion" + "FS" | Do not confuse with assurance (broader) |
| Audit committee composition | Must be majority independent NEDs | Executive directors ≠ independent |
| Internal vs External audit | Independence is key differentiator | Internal audit can be outsourced – still independent of management |
| Appointment | First = directors; subsequent = shareholders | Casual vacancy = directors can fill |
| Auditor rights | Access to records + attend meetings | Rights apply to officers, not just directors |

## Section B scoring table

| "Describe the content of an engagement letter" | List items: scope, basis, responsibilities, fees, limitations. 1 mark per item. |
| "Explain auditor's rights" | Statutory rights: access, information, attend AGM. 1 mark per right with brief explanation. |

## Part B · Beech & Co
Constructed response – June 2015 style · 10 marks · approx. 18 minutes.

<div class="b-card x-cr" style="margin-top:.6rem"><p><b>Scenario.</b> Beech &amp; Co is a firm of Chartered Certified Accountants. The firm has recently been approached by Sycamore Ltd to perform its statutory audit. The current auditor of Sycamore Ltd resigned following a disagreement with management.</p></div>

<ConstructedResponse client:visible id="c-cp1" crKey="cr-beech-a" title="Beech & Co · (a) Pre-acceptance steps" marks={6} minutes={11}
  requirement="(a) Explain the steps Beech & Co should take BEFORE accepting the audit engagement with Sycamore Ltd. (6 marks)" answer={[
  { heading: 'Pre-acceptance steps (6 marks – 1 mark per point)', points: [
    'Assess own competence and resources – does Beech & Co have sufficient technical expertise and staff to perform the audit of Sycamore Ltd?',
    'Assess ethical issues – in particular independence. Do any partners/staff have financial interests or close personal relationships with Sycamore Ltd?',
    'Communicate with outgoing auditor – obtain professional clearance. Write to the previous auditor asking if there are any professional reasons why Beech & Co should NOT accept the engagement.',
    'Consider why the previous auditor resigned – management disagreement may indicate a problematic client. Evaluate any integrity concerns.',
    'Obtain a letter of engagement – confirm audit scope, management\'s responsibilities, fee basis, and timetable before work begins.',
    'Ensure the company has complied with legal requirements for the outgoing auditor\'s resignation (statement of circumstances).' ] },
]} />
<ConstructedResponse client:visible id="c-cp1" crKey="cr-beech-b" title="Beech & Co · (b) Rights of the outgoing auditor" marks={4} minutes={7}
  requirement="(b) Explain the rights of Sycamore Ltd's OUTGOING auditor. (4 marks)" answer={[
  { heading: 'Rights of outgoing auditor (4 marks – 1 mark per right)', points: [
    'To receive notice of and attend the general meeting at which they will cease to hold office.',
    'To make a written statement to the company before the general meeting regarding the circumstances of their removal/resignation.',
    'To speak at the general meeting on any part of the business which concerns them as former auditor.',
    'To deposit a statement of circumstances with the company – whether or not the circumstances should be brought to shareholders\' attention.' ] },
]} notes={[
  'Pre-acceptance: Many candidates forgot "professional clearance" (communicate with outgoing auditor). This is 1 guaranteed mark.',
  'Management disagreement: Candidates must highlight this as an integrity/risk flag – not just accept it as routine.',
  'Rights of outgoing auditor: Candidates often confused RIGHTS (what they can do) with OBLIGATIONS (what they must do). The statement of circumstances is an obligation, not just a right.',
]} />
