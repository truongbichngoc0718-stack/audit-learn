<Eyebrow>Why it matters</Eyebrow>
## One balance, three cycles
Inventory is usually crucial to assurance about profit, may be complex and is subject to estimation (Ch 13). Closing inventory is deducted in cost of sales, so **every $1 of overstated inventory is $1 of overstated profit**. Biscore (Branch A) holds no inventory, so this cycle exists only in Branch C.

<Eyebrow>Substantive playbook · the count</Eyebrow>
## Before, during and after the count (ISA 501)
Where inventory is material, attend the count unless impracticable: **evaluate** management's instructions → **observe** the count → **inspect** inventory → **perform test counts** → afterwards, check the final records reflect the count. Attendance can serve as a test of control or a substantive procedure.

| When | Procedures |
|---|---|
| **Planning** | Risks of misstatement · controls · whether proper count instructions exist · timing · perpetual system? · locations, including **third parties** (confirm directly under ISA 505 and/or inspect) · need for an auditor's expert |
| **During** | Observe staff follow instructions · **test counts both ways: floor → sheets (completeness), sheets → floor (existence)**, focusing on high-value items · check damaged, obsolete and slow-moving items are identified, and WIP stage of completion · third-party inventory separately identified · record GRN/GDN last numbers · conclude whether the count is reliable |
| **After** | Trace test counts to final sheets · sequence of sheets · all count records included · records adjusted to counted amounts, differences investigated · **cut-off** with last GRN/GDN numbers · third-party replies · reperform the final computation |

**Continuous (perpetual) counting:** counts through the year; focus on controls; all lines counted at least once a year. Counts before or after the year end need **roll back / roll forward** testing of movements.

<Eyebrow>Cut-off · one boundary, three cycles</Eyebrow>
| Document | Before the year end → include in | After the year end → |
|---|---|---|
| **GRN** (purchase) | Purchases · payables · closing inventory | Not in the FS |
| **GDN** (sale) | Revenue · receivables · **not** in closing inventory | Not in revenue; still in closing inventory |

<Eyebrow>Valuation · IAS 2</Eyebrow>
**Lower of cost and NRV.** Cost = purchase + other costs to bring inventory to its present location and condition (standard costs allowed if they approximate actual). NRV = estimated selling price less costs of completion and costs to sell. Audit aims: the method of determining cost and overhead allocation is appropriate · inventory is at the lower of cost and NRV.

**Auditor's expert (ISA 620):** evaluate competence, capability and objectivity; do not refer to the expert in an unmodified report. **Internal audit (ISA 610):** evaluate and reperform some of the work you use; **direct assistance** is prohibited for significant judgements, higher-risk areas needing more than limited judgement, work internal audit was involved in, and decisions about the internal audit function (e.g. IA may check the ageing, not the adequacy of the allowance).

**Activity 3 · Test counts.** DYL883722G: 125 per records, 50 counted · DYL879239X: blue dye has rubbed onto white shirts · DYL261567L: 87 per records, 88 counted.
<Solution label="Show the slide answer">
DYL883722G could indicate an **overstatement** of inventory. DYL879239X may need writing off if the dye cannot be removed: possible **overstatement**. DYL261567L is a small difference, possibly a counting error, to be looked into for **completeness**.

**Procedures:** inspect count sheets for notes on damaged inventory; enquire whether it has been sold or scrapped since the year end; agree any sales to invoices; otherwise discuss saleability so that inventory is at the lower of cost and NRV · note differences between records and counts; check management followed up and adjusted the ledgers (existence and completeness).
</Solution>

**Activity 4–5 · Cost and NRV.** A breakdown shows raw material, labour and overheads per line; DYL120008P cost **$16** per unit but sold for **$10** after the year end.
<Solution label="Show the slide answer">
**Cost:** recalculate the breakdown; agree the total to the TB and FS · agree raw materials to purchase invoices · agree labour to time sheets · enquire into the overhead basis and compare with prior years · agree overheads to supporting documents and ensure they are attributable to production.

**NRV:** DYL120008P cost exceeds NRV and must be written down, or inventory is overstated. For a sample, agree post year-end selling prices to invoices and compare cost per unit with selling price per unit.
</Solution>

<KeyPrinciple>**Count both ways, cut off on the documents, value at the lower of cost and NRV:** that covers existence, completeness, cut-off and valuation of inventory and cost of sales together.</KeyPrinciple>

<QuickCheck lang="en" q="Material inventory is held at a third-party warehouse. What evidence does ISA 501 require?" short="Confirmation and/or inspection">
Direct confirmation from the third party of quantities and condition (ISA 505), and/or inspection or other procedures, for example if the third party's integrity and objectivity are doubtful.
</QuickCheck>
