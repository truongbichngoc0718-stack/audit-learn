Branch C follows the BISC Audit & Assurance course (Ha Long Giang, FCCA, CPA) and the five Skills Checkpoints (with Nguyen Kien Cuong, ACCA). It runs in English, like the exam. It is **not built chapter by chapter**: the 20 chapters are regrouped the way an experienced auditor uses them, and every page links to its practical twin in Branch A (Vietnamese standards, Biscore data).

<Eyebrow>The method</Eyebrow>
## Learn the structure, then hang the detail on it
| Layer | What you learn | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **C1 · Master Map** | Every account and the double entry that links it to others | One test answers two balances; one misstatement moves several. You stop learning topics twice. |
| **C2 · Operating system** | Six models: risk chain, assertions, evidence/AEIOU, direction, roll-forward, proof in total | The same loop answers every Part B–D requirement; cycles only change the documents. |
| **C3 · Cycle playbooks** | Controls (Ch 9) and substantive tests (Ch 12–17) for the same flow, on one page | Exams test a scenario, not a chapter: controls and balances in one cycle come together. |
| **C4 · Engagement frame** | Before the audit · planning · completion and reporting | Parts A, B and E become three decisions in time order. |
| **C5 · Checkpoints** | Past-exam MCQs, constructed responses with model answers, timed mocks | Retrieval under time pressure is what turns knowledge into marks. |

**Daily loop (about 30 minutes):** 10 min on the [Master Map](/c/1/c1-master-map/) (say each account's twin, direction and three procedures before you click) → 15 min on one cycle playbook or engagement page, doing its drills without looking → 5 min writing one constructed-response answer from a checkpoint. Revisit a page after 1, 3 and 7 days; your drill scores are saved in [Progress](/tien-do/).

<Eyebrow>Exam format</Eyebrow>
## 3 hours (+10 minutes), computer-based, 100 marks
| Section | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Section A · 30% | Three 10-mark case-based questions, each with five objective test questions worth 2 marks | 30 |
| Section B · 70% | 1 × 30-mark question + 2 × 20-mark questions (constructed response) | 70 |

The 30-mark question covers planning & risk assessment, internal control or audit evidence; the 20-mark questions cover broader syllabus areas. Global pass rate Mar-22 to Dec-23: 39%–45%.
