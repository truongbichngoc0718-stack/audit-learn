<Objectives title="Learning outcomes">
- Sequence the completion stages and the auditor's duties before and after the report date
- Classify subsequent events as adjusting or non-adjusting and choose the response
- Evaluate going concern and written representations
- Choose the opinion and the report sections affected, in three steps
</Objectives>

<Eyebrow>The completion roadmap (CP5 §1.2)</Eyebrow>
## Five stages, each feeds the opinion
| Stage | What happens | ISA |
|---|---|---|
| ① Fieldwork complete | Misstatements identified, accumulated, evaluated | ISA 450 |
| ② Final review | Subsequent events · going concern · final analytical procedures · written representations · other information | ISA 560 · 570 · 520 · 580 · 720 |
| ③ Form the opinion | Decision tree on uncorrected misstatements, scope limitations, GC | ISA 700 · 705 · 706 · 701 |
| ④ Issue the report | Sign and date: end of the **active** duty for subsequent events | ISA 700 |
| ⑤ Post-issue | **Passive** duty: act only if new facts come to attention | ISA 560 |

**Uncorrected misstatements (ISA 450):** keep a schedule; consider them individually and in aggregate; ask management to correct; communicate them (material ones individually) to TCWG with prior-period effects; obtain a written representation on them. **Overall review:** acceptable, consistent policies · FS compatible with audit findings · adequate disclosure and classification · statutory compliance · substance over form.

<Eyebrow>Subsequent events · ISA 560 + IAS 10</Eyebrow>
## Did the condition exist at the year end?
**Adjusting** events give evidence of conditions at the year end (court case settled, sale of inventory showing NRV, fraud or error). **Non-adjusting** events reflect conditions that arose after it (dividends declared, fire destroying plant, a major restructuring announced): disclose if material.

| Window | Duty | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Year end → report signed | **Active** | Procedures to find events: management's procedures, board minutes after the year end, latest interim FS, budgets and cash flow forecasts, enquiry of legal counsel and management, written representation |
| Report signed → FS issued | **Passive** | If facts emerge that would have changed the report: discuss with management; if FS amended, extend procedures and issue a new report dated no earlier than the amended FS |
| After FS issued | **Passive + prevent reliance** | Management should reissue; new report with an **Emphasis of Matter** on the reissue. If management refuses: take steps to prevent reliance (legal advice, regulator, statement at the AGM) |

<Eyebrow>Going concern · ISA 570 (Revised)</Eyebrow>
## Two judgements: basis appropriate? material uncertainty?
The FS assume the entity continues for the foreseeable future unless management intends or has no realistic alternative but to liquidate or cease trading; otherwise a **break-up basis**. Management's assessment must cover **at least 12 months from the date of the FS** (not from the report date).

| Indicators (Ch 19 · CP5) | Not indicators (CP5 traps) |
|---|---|
| **Financial:** net liabilities · borrowings near maturity without renewal · withdrawal of creditor support · negative operating cash flows · adverse ratios · substantial losses · dividend arrears · inability to pay creditors or meet loan terms · suppliers move to cash on delivery · no new finance | Long-term loan with no breach · a fall in inventory days (cash cycle improves) |
| **Operating:** intention to liquidate · loss of key management, market, customer, licence or supplier · labour difficulties · supply shortages · strong new competitor | Departure of a single non-finance director · funded reorganisation |
| **Other:** capital or statutory non-compliance · litigation unlikely to be satisfied · adverse law changes · uninsured catastrophes | Routine legal proceedings within insurance cover |

**Procedures:** analyse cash flow, profit and other forecasts and their assumptions · latest interim FS · loan agreement terms and breaches · minutes for financing difficulties · lawyers on litigation · confirm support arrangements and the supporter's ability to pay · plans for unfilled orders · events after the period end · regulatory reports · written representations on plans and their feasibility.

**Written representations (ISA 580):** always for management's responsibilities, complete transactions and subsequent events; required by other ISAs; supplementary evidence only, never a substitute. Dated as close as possible to, but not after, the report date. If refused: discuss, re-evaluate management's integrity, consider the report (pervasive → disclaimer, otherwise qualified).

<Eyebrow>Interactive · the decision tree (CP5 §2)</Eyebrow>
## Three questions, five outcomes
<OpinionDecider client:visible />

**Basic elements of the auditor's report (ISA 700):** title · addressee · opinion · basis for opinion · going concern (if relevant) · emphasis of matter (if relevant) · key audit matters (listed entities) · other matter (if relevant) · responsibilities for the FS (management and TCWG) · auditor's responsibilities · other legal and regulatory requirements (if relevant) · engagement partner · signature · address · date.

| Section | When | Opinion |
|---|---|---|
| Basis for Qualified / Adverse / Disclaimer of Opinion | Any modified opinion; **immediately after the Opinion section**, quantifies the matter | Modified |
| Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern (MURGC) | GC appropriate, material uncertainty, **adequately disclosed** | Unmodified |
| Key Audit Matters (ISA 701) | **Listed** entities only; matters of most significance, with how they were addressed | Unmodified |
| Emphasis of Matter (ISA 706) | A matter already disclosed that the auditor highlights (e.g. reissue of FS) | Unmodified |
| Other Matter (ISA 706) | A matter not in the FS but relevant to users | Unmodified |
| Other Information (ISA 720) | Material inconsistency in the annual report the directors refuse to fix: described here | Unmodified (the FS are right) |

**3-step answer for "discuss the effect on the auditor's report" (5 marks):** ① identify and **quantify** (% of PBT, revenue, assets; material or not) → ② apply the tree (misstatement or inability; pervasive or not, using ISA 705's words) → ③ name the sections that change and where the Basis paragraph goes.

<IfSkipped lang="en">the tree, "material" turns into "adverse" without a pervasiveness test, and a going-concern note that is properly disclosed gets a qualification it never deserved: two of the most common lost marks.</IfSkipped>

<KeyPrinciple>**Quantify, classify, then place:** the number decides material, ISA 705's three tests decide pervasive, and only misstatements or scope limitations ever change the opinion.</KeyPrinciple>

<QuickCheck lang="en" q="$4m misstatement in a single account; PBT $20m; total assets $40m. Material? Pervasive?" short="Material, not pervasive">
20% of PBT and 10% of total assets = material. Confined to a single account, not a substantial proportion of the FS = not pervasive → qualified (CP5 mock Q10).
</QuickCheck>
