   1. OVERVIEW OF CIT
   The definition of PE

   The PE under the CIT Law is defined based on commonly accepted
   definitions under international tax law, which is “a production or
   business establishment through which the foreign enterprises carry out
   all or part of the production/business activities in Vietnam”

    Branch, executive office, factory, workshop, means of transportation; oil/gas
   fields, mines, or other natural resource extraction sites in Vietnam
    Construction sites, construction works, installation or assembly projects
    Service-providing facilities (including consulting services) through employees or
   other organizations/individuals in Vietnam
    Agents acting on behalf of the foreign enterprise
    Representatives in Vietnam who are authorized to sign contracts, or not
   authorized but regularly deliver goods or provide services in Vietnam
    E-commerce or digital platforms through which the foreign enterprise supplies
   goods or services in Vietnam.

   revenue but cannot determine costs or taxable income.
