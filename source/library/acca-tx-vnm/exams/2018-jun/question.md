   3.   Expenses of VND160 million were incurred on negotiating with the angel investor and issuing the additional
   shares.
   4.   Allowances of VND200 million were paid to each of the founding shareholders and of VND150 million to the angel

   (b) Calculate the adjustments EDM JSC should declare in its corporate income tax (CIT) return for the year ended
   31 December 2017 in respect of each of items 1 to 5 above.
