   1. TAX SYSTEM INTRODUCTION
   STRUCTURE OF THE VIETNAMESE TAX SYSTEM
   Overall
   The administrative function for the collection of tax is undertaken by the General
   Department of Taxation and scaled down to Provincial Tax Department and District Tax
   Department (tax authority).

   The tax department staffs are referred to in the tax legislation as Tax officers. They
   are responsibility for:
   • providing guidance to tax payer, supervising the self-assessment system and tax
   filling;
   • agreeing tax liabilities, collecting tax and following up amounts of unpaid tax,
   conducting tax inquiry and inspection.

   The taxpayer is the person & organization responsible for submitting the return and
   for paying whatever tax becomes due.
