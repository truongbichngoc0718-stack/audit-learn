   1. OVERVIEW OF FCT
   Legislation

      Foreign contractor tax (“FCT”) is not a separate type of taxation, instead it is
   a term colloquially referring to the CIT/PIT and VAT obligations of the foreign
   organization and foreign individuals doing business or having income in
   Vietnam (i.e. foreign contractors)

    From 01 July 2025, the VAT-FCT rules in Articles 6, 9, 12 and 15 of Circular
   103/2014/TT-BTC are repealed and replaced by Article 9 of Circular
   69/2025/TT-BTC.
    From CIT period 2025, CIT-FCT rates and computation are governed by Article
   12 Clause 3 of Decree 320/2025/ND-CP.
    Circular 103/2014 remains the reference for scope, taxpayers and procedural
   items not yet superseded.

2. METHOD OF TAX CALCULATION
   VAS method

   “VAS method” is the short name colloquially used to refer to the FCT payment
   for VAT under deduction method and CIT on the actual assessable income basis.

   Foreign contractors and foreign sub-contractors shall pay tax under this method
   if they satisfy all of the following conditions:
   1) The contractor/subcontract or has a permanent establishment (PE) in
   Vietnam or the contractor/ subcontractor is a resident of Vietnam
   2) The period of conducting business in Vietnam under the contracts must be
   at least 183 days from the effective date of the contracts
   3) They register Tax ID number, apply the Vietnamese Accounting System

   bisconline.edu.vn   25

25

   2. METHOD OF TAX CALCULATION
   VAS method

   FCT payable   VAT payable   CIT payable

   VAT calculation
   VAT shall be calculated using the deduction method as provided under VAT laws
   (i.e. output VAT less deductible input VAT).

   CIT calculation
   CIT shall be calculated based on the assessable income of the contracts,
   determined under the CIT laws (i.e. standard case of 20% on assessable income)
