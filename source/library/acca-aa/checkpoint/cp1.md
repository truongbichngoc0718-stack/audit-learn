```text
MINDMAP: Appointment, Removal & Liability (Ch 1)
         TOPIC                                                                  KEY RULES

 Appointment               First auditor: appointed by directors (before first AGM). Subsequent: shareholders at AGM.

 Removal                   By ordinary resolution (shareholders) with special notice (28 days). Auditor can attend AGM & address
                           shareholders.

 Resignation               Auditor must deposit statement of circumstances at company registered office. No suppression of matters
                           shareholders should know.

 Rights of auditor         Access to books/records; receive info from officers; attend/speak at general meetings.

 Liability                 Unlimited liability at law (unless limited liability partnership). Cannot contract out of liability for negligence.

 True & Fair View          Overall FS must present a true and fair view. Auditor must consider this even if individual items comply with IFRS.
```

```text
A. A three-party relationship

D. Sufficient appropriate evidence

  Answer                                                                    Explanation
```

```text
Part B – Constructed Response [40 minutes]
Constructed Response – June 2015 Style (10 marks, ~18 minutes)
SCENARIO: Beech & Co is a firm of Chartered Certified Accountants. The firm has recently been approached by Sycamore Ltd to perform its statutory audit.
The current auditor of Sycamore Ltd resigned following a disagreement with management.

Required:
(a) Explain the steps Beech & Co should take BEFORE accepting the audit engagement with Sycamore Ltd. (6 marks)
(b) Explain the rights of Sycamore Ltd's OUTGOING auditor. (4 marks)

MODEL ANSWER:
(a) Pre-acceptance steps (6 marks – 1 mark per point):
• Assess own competence and resources – does Beech & Co have sufficient technical expertise and staff to perform the audit of Sycamore Ltd?
• Assess ethical issues – in particular independence. Do any partners/staff have financial interests or close personal relationships with Sycamore
Ltd?
```

### Trang 10

```text
• Communicate with outgoing auditor – obtain professional clearance. Write to the previous auditor asking if there are any professional reasons
why Beech & Co should NOT accept the engagement.
• Consider why the previous auditor resigned – management disagreement may indicate a problematic client. Evaluate any integrity concerns.
• Obtain a letter of engagement – confirm audit scope, management's responsibilities, fee basis, and timetable before work begins.
• Ensure the company has complied with legal requirements for the outgoing auditor's resignation (statement of circumstances).
(b) Rights of outgoing auditor (4 marks – 1 mark per right):
• To receive notice of and attend the general meeting at which they will cease to hold office.
• To make a written statement to the company before the general meeting regarding the circumstances of their removal/resignation.
• To speak at the general meeting on any part of the business which concerns them as former auditor.
• To deposit a statement of circumstances with the company – whether or not the circumstances should be brought to shareholders' attention.
 Examiner's Report – Common Mistakes:
    • Pre-acceptance: Many candidates forgot "professional clearance" (communicate with outgoing auditor). This is 1 guaranteed mark.
    • Management disagreement: Candidates must highlight this as an integrity/risk flag – not just accept it as routine.
    • Rights of outgoing auditor: Candidates often confused RIGHTS (what they can do) with OBLIGATIONS (what they must do). The statement of
    circumstances is an obligation, not just a right.
```

```text
M11. Constructed Response – Corporate Governance (10 marks, ~18 minutes)
SCENARIO: Willow Co has recently expanded and is planning to list on the national stock exchange. Currently, the board of directors comprises only
executive directors. The company's legal advisor has recommended establishing an audit committee prior to the listing, but the CEO is unconvinced and does
not understand the committee's role or who should sit on it.
Required:
(a) Explain the recommended composition of an audit committee for a listed company. (2 marks)
(b) Describe FOUR key responsibilities of an audit committee. (8 marks)
MODEL ANSWER:
(a) Composition (2 marks):
• An audit committee should comprise a board sub-committee of independent Non-Executive Directors (NEDs).
• For listed companies, it typically requires at least three independent NEDs, with no executive directors on the committee.
(b) Key responsibilities (8 marks – 2 marks per well-explained point):
• Financial reporting: Monitor the integrity of the financial statements.
• Internal controls: Review the company's internal financial controls and risk management systems.
• Internal audit: Monitor and review the effectiveness of the internal audit function.
• External audit: Make recommendations regarding the appointment, reappointment, and removal of the external auditor, and monitor their independence.
```
