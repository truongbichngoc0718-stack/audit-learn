```text
MINDMAP: Risk Assessment (Ch 6)
Audit Risk = Inherent Risk × Control Risk × Detection Risk

           COMPONENT                                                           DESCRIPTION & EXAM FOCUS

 Inherent Risk (IR)                   Susceptibility of an assertion to misstatement, BEFORE controls. Higher for: complex transactions,
                                      subjective estimates, related party transactions, new accounting standards.

 Control Risk (CR)                    Risk that client's controls do NOT prevent/detect misstatement. Higher when: no segregation of duties,
                                      override of controls, manual systems, poor IT controls.

 Detection Risk (DR)                  Risk that audit procedures fail to detect misstatement. The ONLY component auditor CONTROLS. When
                                      IR/CR high → reduce DR → more work needed.

 Audit Risk Model                     AR = IR × CR × DR → DR = AR / (IR × CR). If IR and CR both high → DR must be set very LOW → extensive
                                      substantive testing.

 Materiality (ISA 320)                Threshold above which misstatements are considered material. % benchmarks: PBT (5%), Revenue (1–
                                      2%), Gross Assets (1–2%). Performance materiality = lower than materiality.

 Business Risk                        Risk to the client entity's objectives. NOT the same as audit risk. Business risks that affect FS reliability
                                      become audit risks.
```

```text
Which of the following factors would MOST likely increase the inherent risk of a company?
A. The company has a highly effective internal audit department
B. The company operates in a rapidly changing, highly regulated tech industry
C. The external auditor has audited the company for the past 5 years
D. The company has strong segregation of duties in its payroll department

              complex, and highly regulated industry naturally increases the risk of errors in the financial statements regardless of internal

Question 8:
Which of the following statements best describes the concept of performance materiality?
A. An amount set by the auditor at less than materiality for the financial statements as a whole
B. The maximum amount of error the auditor is willing to accept in a specific account balance
C. The threshold above which all misstatements must be reported directly to the shareholders
D. The preliminary estimate of materiality based on draft, unaudited financial statements

  Answer                                                                        Explanation

 A            Performance materiality is set at an amount lower than overall materiality. Its purpose is to reduce to an appropriately low level
              the probability that the aggregate of uncorrected and undetected misstatements exceeds overall materiality.
```

```text
Part B – Constructed Response [40 minutes]
Constructed Response – Blackberry Co (Mar/Jun 2018 Style, 20 marks)
SCENARIO: Blackberry Co is a listed company. Revenue declined 30%; significant inventory obsolescence. CEO's bonus is based on profit before tax. New ERP
system introduced during the year. $5m bank loan with covenant: net assets must exceed $8m; current net assets $8.5m.

Required: Identify and explain SIX audit risks from the information above. (12 marks)
Note: 2 marks per risk = 1 mark for identifying the risk factor + 1 mark for the audit risk implication.

MODEL ANSWER:
Risk factor: Revenue decline 30%
Audit risk: Significant decline in revenue may indicate management has recognized revenue incorrectly (accelerated recognition) or has
included fictitious transactions to minimize the decline in reported figures.

Risk factor: Inventory obsolescence
Audit risk: Inventory may be overstated if not written down to NRV (IAS 2). With declining revenue, inventory may not sell at expected prices,
leading to overvaluation.

Risk factor: CEO bonus based on PBT
```

### Trang 12

```text
Audit risk: Management may have incentive to overstate revenue or understate expenses to maximize PBT, increasing the risk of fraudulent
financial reporting (ISA 240).

Risk factor: New ERP system
Audit risk: Data migration from old to new system carries risk of data loss or corruption. Transactions processed during switchover may contain
errors not detected by new controls.

Risk factor: Bank loan covenant (net assets $8.5m vs. $8m threshold)
Audit risk: Risk that management manipulates figures to avoid breaching the covenant. Assets may be overstated or liabilities understated.
Going concern risk if covenant breached and loan recalled.

Risk factor: Listed company / IAS compliance
Audit risk: As a listed entity, Blackberry Co must comply with full IFRS. Risk of non-compliance with disclosure requirements (e.g., related
parties, segments, EPS) is higher.

 Examiner's Report – Common Mistakes:
    • Risk identification (Examiner, 2018–2023): Candidates who score well clearly LINK the scenario fact to the audit implication. "Revenue declined"
    alone = ½ mark. "Revenue declined 30% → management may have recognized fictitious sales to reduce the decline → overstatement of revenue" = 2
    marks.
    • Audit risk ≠ Business risk: Business risk is risk to the company. Audit risk is risk of misstatement in FS. Must translate business events into FS impact.
    • Bonus schemes: ALWAYS flag the risk of earnings management / fraudulent reporting when directors have performance-based bonuses.
    • Loan covenants: ALWAYS identify both the risk of misstatement AND the going concern risk if covenant is close to breach.
```

```text
Elm plc has requested that Cedar & Co assist with the preparation of their corporate tax return this year.
Required:
Identify and explain the ethical threats arising from the matters above, and recommend appropriate safeguards to reduce the threats to an acceptable
level. (4 marks)
MODEL ANSWER:
Matter 1: Engagement partner for eight years (2 marks)
• Threat: This creates a significant familiarity threat, as the partner may become too sympathetic to the client's interests due to the long-standing
relationship.
• Safeguard: For listed entities, the engagement partner must be rotated off the audit after a maximum of seven years. Since the partner has been in place
for eight years, they must immediately step down from the engagement and be replaced by a new partner. A 5-year cooling-off period will then apply.
Matter 2: Preparation of tax return (2 marks)
• Threat: Providing non-audit services such as tax preparation to an audit client creates a self-review threat, as the audit team may be reluctant to identify
errors in work prepared by their own firm.
• Safeguard: The firm should use separate teams for the audit and the tax work. Furthermore, management of Elm plc must take full responsibility for the
tax return.
```
