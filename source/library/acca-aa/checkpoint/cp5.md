```text
1. Obtain a SCHEDULE of the redundancy calculation and cast it to confirm arithmetical accuracy; agree the total to the trial balance and draft FS.

2. Inspect the BOARD MINUTES of the meeting at which the closure decision was taken to confirm the formal decision date (before YE 31 May 20X5).

3. Obtain a copy of the PRESS RELEASE dated 28 May 20X5 announcing the closures, and the COMMUNICATION to affected staff, to confirm the constructive obligation
under IAS 37 crystallised before YE.

4. For a sample of affected employees, recalculate the individual redundancy entitlement using the company redundancy policy + length of service + salary records, and
reperform the calculation; agree to HR records.

5. Obtain WRITTEN REPRESENTATION from management confirming that all affected employees have been included, the basis of calculation is consistent with the
company policy, and no further reorganisation provisions are required at YE.

6. Review POST-YE payments to confirm actual redundancy payments are consistent with the calculation, providing further evidence on completeness and valuation.
```
