2. TESTS OF CONTROLS
 The auditor will perform tests of controls to
gather audit evidence about relevant financial
statement assertions if their initial risk
assessment suggests that the entity's internal
controls operate effectively
 The auditor must test that the control:
- Is properly designed;
- Exists; and
- Has operated throughout the period

301

2. TESTS OF CONTROLS
Tests of controls include enquiry in combination with other audit
procedures, for example:
(a) Inspection of documents supporting controls or events
(b) Observation of the entity's control procedures, for example
observing an inventory count
(c) Re-performance of the application of a control to ensure it was
performed correctly
(d) Examination of evidence of management reviews, for example
minutes of board meetings
(e) Testing of the control activities performed by a computer, using for
example computer- assisted audit techniques (CAATs)

The payroll system has two main areas to consider:
Human Resources (HR) Function
Includes:
- Staff appointment
- Staff removal
- Staff appraisal
- Notifications of salary changes

Payroll processing function

Includes:
Monthly processing of payroll

These two distinct roles would be carried out by different members of
staff/ departments, however, this may not be possible in smaller
businesses where we have seen that it can be difficult to have sufficient
segregation of duties.

• Claiming payment for more hours

• Theft (if wages are paid in cash
(rare))

Errors

• Other deductions
